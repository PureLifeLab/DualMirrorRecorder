DualMirrorRecorder Privacy Policy

生效日期：2026年5月28日

Effective Date: May 28, 2026

本隐私政策适用于 DualMirrorRecorder（以下简称「本应用」），由PureLifeLab开发并维护。

This Privacy Policy applies to DualMirrorRecorder (hereinafter referred to as "the App"), developed and maintained by PureLifeLab.

1. 我们收集的信息

1. Information We Collect

本应用遵循「最小必要」原则，仅收集保障核心功能运行的必要信息：

The App follows the principle of "minimum necessary" and only collects essential information to ensure the operation of core functions:

  • 设备基础信息：设备型号、iOS系统版本、唯一设备标识符（IDFV），用于适配设备、修复崩溃、优化运行体验
  • Basic Device Information: Device model, iOS version, Identifier for Vendor (IDFV), used for device adaptation, crash fixing, and experience optimization.
  • 使用数据：功能使用频率、操作日志（匿名化处理），用于分析产品使用情况、优化功能体验
  • Usage Data: Function usage frequency, operation logs (anonymized), used to analyze product usage and optimize features.
  • 拍摄内容：录制的视频、照片仅存储于用户本地设备，不会上传至服务器，完全由用户自主掌控
  • Captured Content: Recorded videos and photos are stored locally on the user’s device only, never uploaded to servers, fully controlled by the user.

2. 权限说明

2. Permissions

  • 相机权限：用于前后置摄像头同时预览与双摄录制
  • Camera: Used for front/back camera preview and dual-camera recording.
  • 麦克风权限：用于视频录制时同步录音
  • Microphone: Used for audio recording during video capture.
  • 相册/存储权限：用于保存视频至本地相册及导出
  • Photos/Storage: Used to save recorded videos to your photo library and export.

3. 信息使用目的

3. Purposes of Information Use

  • 提供、维护和优化双摄录制、视频处理与分享功能
  • Provide, maintain and optimize dual-camera recording, video processing and sharing features.
  • 排查应用崩溃问题，保障服务稳定运行
  • Troubleshoot app crashes and ensure stable service operation.
  • 遵守相关法律法规要求
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations.

4. 信息存储与安全

4. Information Storage and Security

用户拍摄的视频/照片100%存储于本地设备，不上传至任何第三方服务器；设备信息与使用数据仅通过加密方式传输至官方分析服务，采用行业标准安全措施保护，防止数据泄露、篡改。

User videos/photos are 100% stored locally and not uploaded to any third-party servers. Device and usage data are transmitted to official analytics services only via encrypted channels, protected by industry-standard security measures to prevent data leakage and tampering.

我们不会向任何第三方共享、出售、出租用户的个人信息，法律法规要求除外。

We will not share, sell or rent users’ personal information to any third parties except as required by laws and regulations.

5. 用户权利

5. User Rights

  • 自主管理、删除本地存储的所有视频与照片
  • Independently manage and delete all locally stored videos and photos.
  • 查询、更正、删除本应用收集的个人信息
  • Inquire, correct or delete personal information collected by the App.
  • 撤回数据收集同意，限制数据处理
  • Withdraw consent to data collection and restrict data processing.

6. 儿童隐私

6. Children’s Privacy

本应用面向全年龄段用户（含4岁及以上儿童），不会刻意收集13岁以下儿童的个人身份信息。

The App is intended for users of all ages (including children aged 4 and above). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

7. Updates to the Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy due to product updates or regulatory requirements. After updates, we will notify users within the App prominently. Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
