双视相机 隐私政策

DualMirrorRecorder Privacy Policy

生效日期：2025年12月29日

Effective Date: December 29, 2025

本隐私政策适用于 双视相机（DualMirrorRecorder，以下简称「本应用」）。本应用是一款纯本地双摄像头视频录制工具，无云端服务、无后台服务器、无数据上传。

This Privacy Policy applies to DualMirrorRecorder (hereinafter referred to as "the App"). The App is a pure local dual-camera recording tool with no cloud services, no backend servers, and no data uploading.

1. 我们收集的信息

1. Information We Collect

本应用严格遵循完全离线、零收集、零上传原则，不会主动收集任何个人信息：

The App strictly follows the principles of 100% offline, zero collection, zero upload. We do NOT collect any personal information:

  • 相机与视频数据：所有录制的视频、预览画面、拍摄内容仅存储在用户本地设备，不会上传、不会同步、不会被访问
  • Camera & Video Data: All recorded videos, preview frames, and captured content are stored locally only, never uploaded or accessed externally.
  • 相册数据：仅在用户手动授权后访问本地相册，用于保存视频与回放，不会读取、上传任何相册内容
  • Photo Library Data: Accesses local album only after user authorization for saving and playback; no reading or uploading of any album content.
  • 设备信息：本应用不收集设备型号、系统版本、IDFA、IDFV 或任何设备标识
  • Device Information: The App does NOT collect device model, iOS version, IDFA, IDFV, or any device identifiers.
  • 使用数据与日志：不收集操作日志、使用行为、崩溃日志、分析数据，无任何统计服务
  • Usage Data & Logs: No operation logs, usage data, crash logs, or analytics are collected.

2. 信息使用目的

2. Purposes of Information Use

  • 仅用于在用户设备上运行相机录制、视频预览、保存、回放等核心功能
  • Only used to run core functions such as camera recording, video preview, saving, and playback on the user’s device.
  • 不用于广告、分析、统计、推送或任何其他目的
  • Not used for advertising, analytics, tracking, push notifications, or any other purposes.
  • 遵守当地法律法规与 iOS 系统权限规则
  • Comply with local laws and iOS system permission policies.

3. 信息存储与安全

3. Information Storage and Security

用户所有视频、图片、配置 100% 本地存储，不上传任何服务器。

All user videos, images, and settings are stored 100% locally and never uploaded to any servers.

本应用无网络权限、无第三方 SDK、无广告、无分析、无后台上传行为，用户数据完全由用户自己掌控。

The App has no internet permission, no third-party SDKs, no ads, no analytics, and no background uploading. User data is fully controlled by the user.

我们不会向任何第三方共享、出售、出租、披露用户数据，除非法律强制要求。

We will not share, sell, rent, or disclose user data to any third parties unless required by law.

4. 系统权限说明

4. System Permissions

  • 相机权限：用于前后摄像头录制与实时预览，拒绝后仍可使用应用其他功能
  • Camera Permission: Used for dual-camera recording and preview; can be denied without breaking other features.
  • 麦克风权限：用于录制视频声音，拒绝后可继续录制无声视频
  • Microphone Permission: Used for recording audio; silent video recording is still available if denied.
  • 相册权限：用于将视频保存到系统相册，拒绝后视频仍可在应用内查看
  • Photo Library Permission: Used for saving videos to the system album; videos can still be viewed inside the App if denied.

5. 用户权利

5. User Rights

  • 完全掌控所有本地视频数据，可随时删除、导出、管理
  • Full control over all local video data; can delete, export, and manage at any time.
  • 随时在系统设置中关闭相机、麦克风、相册权限
  • Disable camera, microphone, and photo library permissions anytime in iOS Settings.
  • 卸载应用即可永久清除所有本地数据
  • Uninstalling the App will permanently erase all local data.

6. 隐私政策更新

6. Updates to the Privacy Policy

本应用如因功能更新导致隐私政策发生变化，将在应用内更新本页面，不会额外收集用户数据。

If the Privacy Policy is updated due to App improvements, the updated version will be posted within the App. No additional data will be collected.
